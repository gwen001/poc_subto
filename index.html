What Is a Subdomain Takeover?

A Subdomain Takeover occurs when a DNS record references a cloud or third-party service that is no longer claimed, configured, or maintained.

If that resource becomes available again, an attacker may be able to register it and gain control over the associated subdomain without compromising any internal infrastructure.

Potential Impact

  • Brand impersonation
  • High-credibility phishing campaigns
  • Hosting of unauthorized content
  • Session trust abuse
  • Email and reputation risks
  • Customer confidence degradation
  • Abuse of domain-based trust relationships

Remediations

Remove Orphaned DNS

Delete DNS entries that point to services as soon as they are no longer in use.

Review Third-Party Services

Verify ownership of all referenced cloud and SaaS resources.

Maintain Asset Inventory

Keep an accurate inventory of active subdomains and dependencies.

Continuous Monitoring

Perform automated checks for abandoned resources and dangling DNS records.
